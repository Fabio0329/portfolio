## How I got here

My path into software wasn't the traditional one. It's been a winding road,
powered by a real love of tech, code, and figuring out how systems fit
together.

Along the way I learned that no matter what we build, it starts and ends with
people: the team building the system, and the customers counting on it. I
served in the Arizona Army National Guard, then spent six years at Apple,
first leading retail teams, then troubleshooting on the IS&T help line, and
finally as a network engineer in Apple's data centers. Every one of those jobs
came down to the same thing: understand what people need, then make sure they
get it.

What I've learned about myself is that I'm a bit of a rare breed: an
extroverted software engineer. I'm at my best working between the business
and the engineers, using years of leading teams and helping customers to get
a clear picture of what's needed, then delivering it. That's exactly what AI
engineering calls for today: someone with a foot in both worlds who can own a
problem, and its solution, end to end.

Since 2021, I've applied that experience across several software engineering
roles, building and maintaining applications from frontend interfaces to
backend services and infrastructure.

- **Infosys:** I built React and Redux applications, added automated UI and
  unit tests, and helped keep our Jenkins CI/CD pipelines running.
- **Opendoor:** I built full-stack inventory management dashboards with React,
  TypeScript, Node.js, and PostgreSQL. I also founded and led the inventory
  team, which helped streamline our internal operations.
- **PPLSI (LegalShield):** I built and maintained enterprise CRM applications
  with React, TypeScript, and REST APIs, working closely with product managers
  and stakeholders to ship new features. I led our frontend modernization to
  cut technical debt and improve reliability, contributed to our
  microservices-based backend, and dug into production issues when they came
  up.

Now I'm moving toward Forward Deployed Engineering, where all of that comes
together: sitting with a customer, understanding what they actually need, and
shipping the AI-powered tool that solves it. This site, its
[blog](/blog), and its [case studies](/projects) are how I'm showing
that work as it happens.
